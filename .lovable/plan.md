

# Creator Dashboard Upgrade

## What's Changing and Why

The current dashboard is built around a 1-token-per-day market creation limit and shows stats in a flat, single-page layout. With the business model change (unlimited market creation), the token card and countdown timer are now irrelevant. The dashboard needs to become a proper creator command center with clear sections for money, content, and growth.

## New Structure

### Header Area
- "Creator Dashboard" title with subtitle
- Three action buttons: **Create Market** (primary CTA), **View Profile** (links to `/creator/{username}`), **Settings** (gear icon)

### Top Section: Money Overview (2 cards side-by-side)
- **Earnings** card: Total earnings with period selector (1D/7D/30D/90D/ALL), net profit calculation, and a note "Auto-withdraw every Friday 12AM EST"
- **Available Balance** card: Current withdrawable balance, Withdraw button, and next payout countdown timer

### Quick Stats Strip (4 metrics in a row)
- **Markets Created** (total count)
- **Total Pot Generated** (using pari-mutuel terminology)
- **Avg Pot / Market**
- **Rank** (with trophy icon)

### Tabbed Content Area (3 tabs)

**Overview Tab**
- Followers card with growth percentage
- Views card with growth percentage  
- Resolution rate (resolved/total as a percentage)
- Top performing market (highest pot) as a highlight card
- Recent activity feed (last 5 market events: created, resolved, disputed)

**Markets Tab**
- Filter bar: All / Open / Resolved / Disputing / Cancelled (with counts)
- Sort: Newest / Pot Size / Players
- Market rows showing: status indicator, title, date, player count, pot size, creator earnings, status badge
- "Create New Market" CTA at bottom
- Empty state per filter

**Analytics Tab**
- Earnings over time (simple bar chart or summary by period)
- Pot generated by period
- Player engagement: total unique players, repeat player rate
- Top 3 markets by pot size
- Top 3 markets by player count
- Category breakdown of markets created

## What's Removed
- Market Tokens card (no longer limited)
- Midnight countdown timer
- The `marketTokens` state and `timeUntilRefresh` effect
- Secondary stats row (folded into Overview tab and Quick Stats)

## Technical Details

### File: `src/pages/CreatorDashboard.tsx` (full rewrite)

**Removed state/logic:**
- `marketTokens`, `timeUntilRefresh`, midnight countdown `useEffect`
- Flat stats grid layout

**New state:**
- `earningsPeriod` for period selector (1d/7d/30d/90d/all)
- `activeTab` via Tabs component (overview/markets/analytics)
- `marketFilter` and `marketSort` (carried over but enhanced with "disputing" filter)

**New mock data:**
- `earningsByPeriod` object with earnings/fees per period
- `recentActivity` array (event log: "Created market X", "Resolved market Y as Yes", etc.)
- `analyticsData` with top markets, category breakdown, player stats
- Enhanced `recentMarkets` with `players` field (renamed from `traders`) and `pot` (renamed from `volume`)

**New imports:**
- `Tabs, TabsContent, TabsList, TabsTrigger` from ui/tabs
- `Eye, ExternalLink, Activity, Zap, PieChart` from lucide-react
- `Progress` from ui/progress

**Key UI patterns:**
- Period selector buttons (same pattern as Portfolio winnings selector)
- Tab badges showing counts
- Market rows reuse the existing card-row pattern but with "pot" and "players" labels
- Analytics cards use simple stat displays with ranked lists
- "View Profile" button navigates to `/creator/sarah-chen` (mock username)
- Auto-withdraw notice replaces the manual "Withdraw" button pattern
